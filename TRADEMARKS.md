or affiliation.

## Scope

This policy applies to the name **KDBX Fortress**, the official KDBX Fortress
logo, and other official project branding.

`KDBX` by itself is an established technical term for the KeePass database
format. This policy does **not** claim exclusive rights to the term `KDBX` alone
and does not restrict truthful statements about KDBX compatibility or support.

## Permitted use

Without separate permission, the following uses are allowed:

- factual references to the original project and compatible forks,
- redistribution of unmodified official KDBX Fortress packages,
- references to KDBX Fortress in documentation, reviews, comparisons, and
  security research,
- use of the name to describe origin, for example “fork of KDBX Fortress” or
  “based on KDBX Fortress”,
- truthful statements that software supports or is compatible with the KDBX
  file format.

## Modified or independently published versions

Modified or independently published versions must not create the impression
that they are official KDBX Fortress releases. In particular:

- A fork must use a clearly distinguishable product name.
- The official KDBX Fortress logo and other official project branding must not
  be used as the fork's own application, store, release, or product identity.
- Statements such as “based on KDBX Fortress” are allowed when it remains clear
  that the version is not published, endorsed, or supported by the KDBX
  Fortress project.
- Store listings, screenshots, release graphics, websites, and package metadata
  must not be presented in a way that creates confusion with an official KDBX
  Fortress release.
- Legally required copyright, attribution, provenance, and license notices must
  remain intact.
